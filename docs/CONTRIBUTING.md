# Contributing

Use a branch and a pull request with the reason for the change, affected policy IDs, draft/approval status, and verification. Do not include confidential data or personal information. Confirm any claimed approval through the authorized records process. Keep unresolved decisions visibly bracketed.

Preserve stable route and content IDs. Keep Major as an Administration-only appointment with no training or routine promotion route. Maintain management above Administrator and keep public rules separate from Staff procedures. Do not infer enforcement or promotion authority from proposed training material.

Run node tools/check.cjs and inspect affected desktop, mobile, keyboard, and print flows. Test search and copy output after content changes. Avoid unnecessary dependencies. Confirm the licensing decision before granting reuse rights.
